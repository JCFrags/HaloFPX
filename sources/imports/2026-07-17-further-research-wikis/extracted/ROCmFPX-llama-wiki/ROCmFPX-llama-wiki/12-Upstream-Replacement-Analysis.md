## Central finding

Upstream has absorbed the fork’s generic HY3/MTP model contribution and continued evolving the surrounding speculative, RPC, server, and platform code. The fork should therefore preserve **capabilities**, not old implementations: custom formats and kernels remain; generic model/platform copies go. ## Replacement test

A fork patch is eligible for retirement when all three conditions hold:

1. a pinned current upstream source or commit implements the same generic responsibility;
2. no fork-only format, hardware, policy, or storage invariant remains in that hunk;
3. current fork-specific acceptance tests pass without the patch.

The first two conditions are evidenced in this inventory; the third must be demonstrated during migration with the retained CI/test suite. ## No false equivalence

Upstream `Q2_0`, generic MTP, or generic RPC should not be treated as replacements for ROCmFPX’s Q2 byte format, strict verification policy, or cache state merely because the names are similar. Replacement decisions are based on byte-level and behavioral equivalence, not naming.
