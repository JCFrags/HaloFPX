# Literal upstream source-range captures

These files preserve selected literal ranges from exact candidate commits used to support fixture coverage, exclusion, and applicability claims. They are evidence only and were not compiled or executed. The normative source path, commit, Git blob SHA-1, requested line range, local SHA-256, license, and access date are recorded in `index.json`.

The captures are redistributed under the MIT license text identified per record. They do not include model weights or tokenizer binaries.
