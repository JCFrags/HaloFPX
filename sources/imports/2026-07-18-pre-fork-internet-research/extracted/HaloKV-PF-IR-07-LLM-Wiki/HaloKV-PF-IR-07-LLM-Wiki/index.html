Required

Authenticated object layer

Canonical AAD binds authority, tenant, project, prefix, namespace, sharing class, rank, epoch, format, codec, chunk, CID, and manifest generation. Authentication precedes decompression and parsing.

AEAD and nonce design →

Scoped privacy

Tenant/project dedup boundaries

Private content uses a scope-specific keyed CID. Public/system prefixes use a separate publisher authority and cannot be populated by silent cross-tenant equality.

Operational

Key lifecycle and recovery

Rank/epoch separation constrains nonce state and blast radius. Busy files, mappings, kernel keyrings, process memory, backups, escrow, and restored snapshots remain explicit dependencies.

Deletion and media

Logical deletion, cryptographic erasure, discard, and physical-media sanitization are separate outcomes with separate evidence. No universal secure-delete or compliance guarantee is asserted.

The bundle contains a clearly marked derivative extract. The cryptsetup release tarball hash is recorded but was not independently signature-verified in this run.

Human decisions still required

The source-backed profile does not choose the principal authority, sharing policy, public/system publisher, key owner/custodian, escrow, retention, rollback tolerance, or physical-media disposition authority. The human-decision ledger names the required artifacts and safe defaults.

Reproducibility

Raw primary documents, exact revisions and access dates, license notices, text extracts, claim labels, CSV/JSON matrices, SHA-256 manifests, and an offline verifier are included. Run python3 validation/verify_bundle.py from the folder root after extraction.
