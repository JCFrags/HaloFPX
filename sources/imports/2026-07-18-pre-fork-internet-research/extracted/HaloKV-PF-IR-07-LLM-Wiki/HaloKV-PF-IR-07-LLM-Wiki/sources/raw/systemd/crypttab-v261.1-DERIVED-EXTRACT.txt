Selected source-faithful facts used in the analysis
---------------------------------------------------
* /etc/crypttab describes encrypted block devices set up during system boot.
* Unlock material may be entered interactively, read from a file, requested
  through an AF_UNIX socket, or obtained through PKCS#11, FIDO2, or TPM2 flows.
* noauto can suppress automatic activation. nofail can allow boot to continue
  when activation fails. headless disallows interactive credential queries.
* password-cache controls temporary caching of interactively entered passwords
  or PINs by systemd's password-agent infrastructure.
* link-volume-key can link a LUKS2 volume key into a kernel keyring; the source
  warns that the key is not automatically removed when the device is detached.
* discard passes discard requests through and the source states that it has
  security implications.
