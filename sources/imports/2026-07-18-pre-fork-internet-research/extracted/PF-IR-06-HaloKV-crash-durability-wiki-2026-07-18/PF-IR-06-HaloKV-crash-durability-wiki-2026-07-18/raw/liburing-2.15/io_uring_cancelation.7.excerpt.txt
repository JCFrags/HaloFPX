When a cancellation is submitted, two CQEs are generated: one for the target
and one for the cancel request. Their order is not guaranteed. The cancel CQE
can report 0, -ENOENT, or -EALREADY; the target can still complete normally,
fail independently, or report cancellation.

Closing the application's file descriptor does not automatically cancel pending
io_uring requests, because the ring holds its own file reference.

Closing the ring cancels pending requests, but explicit cancellation and CQE
draining are needed when results must be processed or resources freed safely.
Disk I/O already submitted to hardware is typically not cancelable.
