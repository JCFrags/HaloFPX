Linked requests execute sequentially. With IOSQE_IO_LINK, a negative result
breaks the chain and later requests complete with -ECANCELED. With
IOSQE_IO_HARDLINK, the chain continues after failure.

Each request in a linked chain has its own CQE. CQEs are ordered within the
chain, although unrelated completions can interleave.

A linked chain must be submitted together. Cancellation of a chain member
breaks a soft-linked chain as though that request failed.
