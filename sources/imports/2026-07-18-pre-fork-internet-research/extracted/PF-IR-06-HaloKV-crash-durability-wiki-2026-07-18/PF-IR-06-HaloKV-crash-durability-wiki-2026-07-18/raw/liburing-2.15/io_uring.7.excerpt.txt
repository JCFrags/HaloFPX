For each SQE, once processing completes, the kernel normally places a matching
CQE in the CQ. The CQE res field contains the equivalent system-call return
value, or the negated errno on failure.

I/O requests can complete in any order. Requests are attempted in submission
order, but that does not imply execution or completion order.

IOSQE_IO_LINK can enforce execution order for requests submitted in one batch.
Exceptions to one-CQE-per-SQE include CQE_SKIP_SUCCESS and multishot operations.
