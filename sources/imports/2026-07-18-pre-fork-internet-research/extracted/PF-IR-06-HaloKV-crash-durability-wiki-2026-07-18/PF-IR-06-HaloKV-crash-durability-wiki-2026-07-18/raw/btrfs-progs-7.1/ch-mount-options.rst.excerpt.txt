Most mount options apply to the whole filesystem and only options in the first
mounted subvolume take effect.

barrier is on by default. Disabling it can lead to filesystem corruption after
a crash or power loss unless a nonvolatile, battery-backed cache supplies the
required guarantees.

nodatacow implies nodatasum and disables compression. In-place updates can
improve performance at the cost of potential partial writes after interruption.

The periodic commit interval controls accumulated unwritten state. flushoncommit
forces prior dirty data into the current commit. Neither option replaces the
application's explicit file and directory synchronization protocol.

discard=async batches TRIM. fstrim is an alternative. ENOSPC debugging is an
administrative aid, not an allocation guarantee.
