Publication ordering

root lock
  → parent dir fd
  → O_TMPFILE (probed) or named O_EXCL temp
  → optional fallocate
  → exact offset writes + format/hash validation
  → fdatasync/fsync(temp)
  → linkat / renameat2 (same filesystem)
  → fsync(parent directory)
  → acknowledgement
Atomic rename is not durable publication. The directory synchronization step remains explicit. A write CQE is not a persistence boundary, and cancellation does not roll back storage I/O.
