Decision state

The pack unblocks watch-list and evidence-collection decisions. It does not authorize firmware rollout or hardware-service thresholds.

BIOS rollout remains on hold. The OEM support page does not publish an exact MME3L/board-v1.0 BIOS package. The measured BIOS date precedes AMD's 1.0.0.2a and 1.0.0.2b release-to-OEM dates. Exact PI integration, signing and rollback remain unproven.
