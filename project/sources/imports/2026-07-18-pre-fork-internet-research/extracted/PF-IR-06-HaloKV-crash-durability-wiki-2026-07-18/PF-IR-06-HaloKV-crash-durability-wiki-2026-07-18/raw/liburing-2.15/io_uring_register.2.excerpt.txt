Registration gives the kernel long-term references to files or long-term
mappings of application memory. Registered buffers are locked/pinned for fixed
buffer operations. Resource updates may return before old resources are
actually deallocated; they can remain alive until all users complete.

When nonzero resource tags are used, a CQE carrying the tag is posted after the
resource is unregistered and no longer in use.

Registered files are automatically unregistered on ring teardown. Buffer
unpinning during shutdown may proceed asynchronously.
