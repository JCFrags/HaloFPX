# Quality qualification gate

No selected quantization has been quality-qualified. Phase 2 must compare each exact artifact with an approved reference on the user’s workload. At minimum, record deterministic prompt sets, tokenizer/template identity, logit/KL or perplexity deltas where feasible, tool-schema validity, long-context retrieval, agent-loop success, and human preference/error severity. Aggressive two-bit artifacts require especially explicit acceptance thresholds. A conversion publisher’s benchmark is not a substitute for local qualification.
