# Literal claim labels

- **`VERIFIED-SOURCE`** — Exact repository, commit, path, or publisher record observed at the access date.
- **`VERIFIED-LICENSE-TEXT`** — Exact license text captured from the pinned source revision.
- **`SELF-GENERATED`** — Bytes or records produced solely by a deterministic CC0 recipe in this folder.
- **`UNEXECUTED-EVIDENCE`** — Source or command evidence retained but not executed during preparation.
- **`QUALIFICATION-REQUIRED`** — Not accepted for live candidate execution until isolated local qualification succeeds.
- **`PROPOSAL`** — A proposed applicability or oracle decision, not an approved test policy.
- **`INFERENCE`** — A conclusion derived from cited primary records rather than directly stated by one record.
- **`EXCLUDED-LICENSE-UNCLEAR`** — Storage/execution rights for the exact asset were not established.
- **`EXCLUDED-PROVENANCE-GAP`** — An exact byte artifact was found, but its source/conversion/license chain was insufficient.
- **`UNVERIFIED-IDENTITY`** — The named candidate could not be tied to an authoritative exact repository/revision.
- **`OPEN-QUESTION`** — Requires local source, publisher, or owner evidence before disposition.
