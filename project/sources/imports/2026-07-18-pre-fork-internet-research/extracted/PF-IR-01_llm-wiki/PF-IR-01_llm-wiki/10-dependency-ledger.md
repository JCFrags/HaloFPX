## Other bundled dependencies

[VERIFIED] The official nlohmann/json, stb, and miniaudio repository security pages showed no published advisories at the access date.

[OPEN] “No published advisory” is not equivalent to “no vulnerability.” stb and miniaudio direct reports through public issue workflows, and subprocess.h did not have a verified official advisory program in the captured research.

[RECOMMENDATION] Pin each vendored file to an upstream tag/commit and blob hash, refresh advisories per release, and include the exact vendored blob in the SBOM.

## System and non-bundled libraries

[OPEN] OpenSSL, ROCm/HIP runtime, GPU driver, libc, libstdc++, compression libraries, and the container/OS base are deployment inputs. Their versions, CVEs, and loaded-file hashes remain local provenance and must be evaluated by the platform release gate.
