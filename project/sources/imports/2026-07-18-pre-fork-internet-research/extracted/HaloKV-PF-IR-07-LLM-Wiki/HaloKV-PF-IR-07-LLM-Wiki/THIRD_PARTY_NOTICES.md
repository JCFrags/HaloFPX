# Third-party notices

The `sources/` subtree contains or references primary-source material from the
Linux kernel project, cryptsetup, LUKS2-docs, systemd, NIST, and the IETF.
Those materials remain under their own licenses and embedded notices.

The source catalog records exact document revisions, release tags or commits,
access dates, local file paths, and SHA-256 values. A blank `local_sha256`
means the record is a reference-only or aggregate record; it does not mean the
source has been verified. The systemd XML archival exception and the unverified
cryptsetup release-archive hash are stated explicitly in the corresponding raw
notes and source catalog.

validation, FIPS status, certification, or compliance.
